Dexter-CLAI Realtor Assistant

Dexter-CLAI is a private business productivity application used by Dexter Joseph to assist with real estate relationship management, contact follow-ups, and business communications.

Google Services Integration

Dexter-CLAI integrates with Google services with the account owner's authorization. Google Gmail access is used to send authorized business email communications on behalf of the account owner.

Dexter-CLAI does not provide public access to the underlying automation system.
