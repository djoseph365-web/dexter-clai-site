Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Dexter-CLAI Realtor Assistant ("Dexter-CLAI") accesses and uses information in connection with its business productivity functions.

About Dexter-CLAI

Dexter-CLAI is a private business productivity application used by Dexter Joseph for real estate relationship management, contact follow-ups, and authorized business communications.

Google Account Access

Dexter-CLAI may connect to Google services through Google's OAuth authorization system. Access occurs only after the Google account owner grants the requested permissions.

The application currently requests Gmail permission for the purpose of sending authorized business email messages on behalf of the account owner.

How Google User Data Is Used

Google user data accessed through Dexter-CLAI is used only to provide the application's authorized business communication functions.

Dexter-CLAI does not sell Google user data or use Google user data for advertising purposes.

Data Sharing

Dexter-CLAI does not sell Google user data. Information is shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the application's authorized functionality or when required by law.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information processed by Dexter-CLAI. Access to the underlying automation system is restricted.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for the application's business purposes. The account owner may revoke Dexter-CLAI's Google account access through their Google Account security settings.
